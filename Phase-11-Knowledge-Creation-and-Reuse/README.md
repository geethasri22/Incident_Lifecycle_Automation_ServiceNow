# Phase 11 — Knowledge Creation, Approval & Publication

## 📌 Overview

This phase demonstrates how the successful resolution of an Incident can be converted into reusable organizational knowledge.

During the previous phases, the corporate VPN connectivity Incident was investigated and resolved through a network configuration change.

Instead of allowing the resolution information to remain only within the Incident record, the solution is documented as a new Knowledge Article.

The article is then reviewed, approved, and published so that support teams can use it for future VPN-related incidents.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objectives of this phase are:

- Create a new Knowledge Article.
- Document the successful VPN resolution.
- Provide reusable troubleshooting information.
- Submit the article for approval.
- Approve the Knowledge Article.
- Publish the article.
- Verify that the published article can be found in the Knowledge Base.

---

# 🔄 Knowledge Creation Workflow

The Knowledge Management workflow is:

Resolved Incident
       ↓
Successful Resolution Identified
       ↓
Knowledge Article Created
       ↓
Article Review
       ↓
Approval
       ↓
Publication
       ↓
Knowledge Available for Reuse
