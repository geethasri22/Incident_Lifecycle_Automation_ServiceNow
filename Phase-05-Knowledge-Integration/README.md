# Phase 5 — Knowledge Integration

## 📌 Overview

This phase demonstrates the integration of Knowledge Management with the Incident Management process.

The VPN connectivity Incident created and classified in the previous phases is investigated using an existing Knowledge Article.

The purpose of using Knowledge Management is to provide support engineers with standardized troubleshooting instructions before escalating the Incident to a specialized support team.

For this project, the existing article:

**KB0010002 — VPN Connection Troubleshooting**

is used to troubleshoot the reported VPN connectivity issue.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objectives of this phase are:

- Access the ServiceNow Knowledge Base.
- Search for relevant troubleshooting information.
- Locate the VPN troubleshooting Knowledge Article.
- Review the recommended troubleshooting steps.
- Use the Knowledge Article during Incident investigation.
- Determine whether the existing troubleshooting information resolves the issue.
- Establish the basis for further escalation if the issue persists.

---

# 🧑‍💻 Business Scenario

The Incident concerns an employee who is unable to connect to the corporate VPN.

The Incident has already been:

- Created
- Classified as Network → VPN
- Assigned Impact 2 – Medium
- Assigned Urgency 2 – Medium
- Assigned Priority 3 – Moderate

Before escalating the issue to Level 2 Network Support, the support team checks the Knowledge Base for an existing solution.

---

# 📚 Knowledge Management

ServiceNow Knowledge Management provides a centralized location for storing troubleshooting information, known solutions, procedures, and technical guidance.

Instead of independently troubleshooting every Incident, support agents can search existing Knowledge Articles and follow documented procedures.

The workflow is:
VPN Incident
     ↓
Search Knowledge Base
     ↓
Find VPN Troubleshooting Article
     ↓
Review Troubleshooting Steps
     ↓
Perform Troubleshooting
     ↓
Issue Persists
     ↓
Escalate to Level 2
