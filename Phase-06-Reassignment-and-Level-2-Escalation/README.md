# Phase 6 — Reassignment & Level 2 Escalation

## 📌 Overview

This phase demonstrates the escalation of the VPN Incident from the initial support level to a specialized Level 2 Network Support team.

During Phase 5, the existing Knowledge Article was reviewed and the recommended troubleshooting steps were performed. Since the VPN connectivity problem was not completely resolved, further technical investigation was required.

The Incident was therefore reassigned to the Network Support team and assigned to the network administrator for Level 2 investigation.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objectives of this phase are:

- Recognize when Level 1 troubleshooting is insufficient.
- Reassign the Incident to a specialized support group.
- Assign the Incident to an appropriate technical resource.
- Document the escalation using Work Notes.
- Maintain a clear Incident audit trail.
- Prepare the Incident for detailed Level 2 investigation.

---

# 🧑‍💻 Escalation Scenario

The Incident concerns a corporate VPN connectivity problem.

The support team first attempted to resolve the issue using the existing Knowledge Article:

**KB0010002 — VPN Connection Troubleshooting**

However, the troubleshooting procedure did not completely resolve the issue.

The Incident therefore requires specialized network-level investigation.
