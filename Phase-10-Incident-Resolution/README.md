# Phase 10 — Incident Resolution

## 📌 Overview

This phase completes the Incident resolution process.

The Incident originated as a corporate VPN connectivity problem. It was classified, investigated, escalated to Level 2 Network Support, and associated with an Emergency Change Request.

After the required corrective action was identified and implemented, the Incident was moved toward resolution.

The resolution information is recorded in ServiceNow to provide a clear record of how the issue was addressed.

## 🎯 Objective

The objectives of this phase are:

- Complete the technical resolution process.
- Update the Child Incident with resolution information.
- Record the resolution code.
- Document the resolution in Resolution Notes.
- Resolve the Parent Incident.
- Verify the final Incident state.
- Maintain a complete Incident lifecycle history.
