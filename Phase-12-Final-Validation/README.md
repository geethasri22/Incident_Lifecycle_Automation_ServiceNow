# Phase 12 — Final Validation & End-to-End Verification

## 📌 Overview

This phase performs the final validation of the ServiceNow Incident Lifecycle Automation project.

The objective is to verify that the complete Incident Management workflow implemented throughout the previous phases operates correctly and that the major ServiceNow records are connected logically.

The project began with the creation and classification of a corporate VPN connectivity Incident and progressed through Knowledge Management, Level 2 escalation, technical investigation, Change Management, Child Incident tracking, resolution, and Knowledge creation.

The final validation confirms that the complete workflow has been successfully implemented.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objectives of this phase are:

- Verify the final Parent Incident.
- Verify the related Child Incident.
- Verify the Emergency Change Request.
- Verify the published Knowledge Article.
- Verify the Incident activity history.
- Confirm that the Incident reached the Resolved state.
- Validate the complete Incident lifecycle.
- Confirm traceability between the different ITSM records.
